# S1067 — Expressions should not be too complex

## Why
An expression chaining many conditional operators (`?:`, `??`, `&&`, `||`) exceeds what a reader can hold in working memory: bugs hide in the branches nobody re-checks, and the next edit breaks one of them silently. Complexity the analyzer flags in a single expression is complexity no reviewer will verify by eye.

## Do this
1. Extract sub-expressions into local variables or private methods with names that state what each piece *means* — the names are the documentation the expression lacked.
2. If the branches represent cases, consider a `switch` expression or a small lookup instead of nested ternaries.
3. Re-check the logic of each extracted piece as you split it — the split is where latent bugs surface.

## AVOID
- Do not just split the expression across multiple lines or into meaningless `temp1`, `temp2` temporaries — same complexity, more scrolling.
- Do not merge conditions with `&&` to reduce the operator count — that trades one unreadable shape for another without naming anything.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
