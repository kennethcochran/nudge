# S1155 — "Any()" should be used to test for emptiness

## Why
`collection.Count() > 0` walks the entire sequence to produce a number you
immediately throw away; `Any()` stops after the first element. On a lazy
pipeline or a database query, `Count()` can materialize or transfer far more
data than the emptiness question requires.

## Do this
1. Replace `.Count() > 0` / `.Count() != 0` with `.Any()`.
2. Replace `.Count() == 0` with `!.Any()`.

## AVOID
- Do not "optimize" by caching `.Count()` in a local variable. You still
  paid the full enumeration to fill it — the cost moved, it did not shrink.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
