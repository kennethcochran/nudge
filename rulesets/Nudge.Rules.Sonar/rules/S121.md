# S121 — Control structures should use curly braces

## Why
`if (ok) DoWork();` without braces is a maintenance trap: the next person to
add a second statement indents it under the `if` and it runs unconditionally.
The dangling-statement bug is one of the most expensive one-line mistakes in
C#.

## Do this
1. Wrap every `if` / `else` / `for` / `foreach` / `while` body in braces, even
   single-statement bodies.
2. Keep the body on its own lines inside the braces.

## AVOID
- Do not dodge the rule by rewriting the branch as a nested ternary or an
  expression-bodied conditional. The branching is still braceless and
  unreadable — and now harder to extend safely. Add the braces.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
