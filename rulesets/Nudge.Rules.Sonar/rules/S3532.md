# S3532 — Empty "default" clauses should be removed

## Why
A `default:` clause containing only `break;` adds a branch that does nothing —
it suggests to readers that the fallthrough case was considered and handled,
when it was merely typed.

## Do this
1. Delete the empty `default:` clause entirely.

## AVOID
- This one is purely mechanical: deletion is the complete fix. Do not keep the
  clause and fill it with a comment (`// nothing to do`) — a commented empty
  branch is still an empty branch. If the do-nothing case genuinely needs
  documenting, that note belongs above the switch, not inside a dead clause.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
