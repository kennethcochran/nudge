# S3366 — "this" should not be exposed from constructors

## Why
Passing `this` out of a constructor — registering with
an event, handing it to another object, calling a virtual method —
exposes a half-built object. The recipient can call back into it before
fields are assigned and invariants established, producing nulls and
inconsistent state that only manifests under specific timing.

## Do this
1. Remove the escape from the constructor: don't register `this`
   with events, don't pass `this` to collaborators, don't call virtual
   members.
2. Do the registration in a factory method or an explicit initialization
   step that runs after construction completes.
3. If the design truly needs two-phase setup, make the second phase
   explicit and unavoidable (the factory calls it; the constructor stays
   private if needed).

## AVOID
- Do not move the leaking call into a private helper method invoked
  from the constructor — the object is still under construction when
  the reference escapes; the helper just hides the leak.

- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
