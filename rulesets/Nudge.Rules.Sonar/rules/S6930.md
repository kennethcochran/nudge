# S6930 — Backslash should be avoided in route templates

## Why
Backslashes in route templates (`[HttpGet("files\{name}")]`) do not act as
path separators in ASP.NET Core routing. The route won't match the URLs you
think it matches, so requests 404 or land on the wrong action — a silent
routing bug.

## Do this
1. Replace `\` with `/` in the route template.
2. If the backslash was meant to match a literal backslash in the URL,
   reconsider: URLs use forward slashes. Handle OS-specific paths inside the
   action body, not in the route.
3. Test that the route matches the intended URL.

## AVOID
- Do not double the backslash (`\\`) to "escape it properly" — an escaped
   backslash is still a backslash in the template and still won't act as a
   separator. Use `/`.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
