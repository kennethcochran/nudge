# S3398 — "private" methods called only by inner classes should be moved to those classes

## Why
A `private` method on the outer class that's only called from the nested class
is in the wrong place — readers look for its callers in the outer class and
find none, and it inflates the outer class's surface for no reason. The method
belongs with its sole caller.

## Do this
1. Move the method into the nested class that calls it.
2. If it needs outer-class state, pass that state as parameters — or
   reconsider whether the nesting is the right design.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by widening to `internal` — accessibility isn't the problem;
  placement is.
- Do not move it to a third helper class nobody asked for — it belongs with
  its sole caller, not in a new home of its own.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
