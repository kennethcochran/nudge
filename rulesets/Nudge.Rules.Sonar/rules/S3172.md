# S3172 — Delegates should not be subtracted

## Why
`d -= handler` on a multicast delegate removes only the *last* matching
invocation and silently does nothing when the handler isn't in the list.
Combined with delegate-equality subtleties (target-plus-method matching, and
lambdas/closures creating a new instance every time), subtraction is an
unreliable way to unsubscribe — handlers leak or the wrong one is removed,
with no error to point at.

## Do this
1. Track handlers explicitly: keep a `List<...>` / `HashSet<...>` of the
   subscribed delegates, add to it on subscribe and remove from it on
   unsubscribe.
2. Alternatively, rebuild the invocation list from the tracked set instead of
   subtracting from the delegate.
3. Test subscribe/unsubscribe cycles, especially with lambdas and closures —
   that's where the silent failures live.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by subtracting in a loop until the delegate is null — that
   is still the same unreliable primitive, just repeated. Manage the handler
   list explicitly.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
