# S1848 — Objects should not be created to be dropped immediately without being used

## Why
`new Foo();` as a bare statement allocates an object nobody references. Either
the constructor's side effect is load-bearing — invisible, untestable, and
fragile — or the line does nothing at all and is pure waste. Both readings
are bad; the statement cannot defend itself.

## Do this
1. If the side effect matters, make it explicit: replace the bare
   construction with a named static method or factory whose name says what
   it does.
2. If nothing matters, delete the statement.

## AVOID
- Do not assign it to a discard (`_ = new Foo();`). The allocation survives,
  the mystery survives — only the diagnostic is gone.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
