# S3900 — Arguments of public methods should be validated against null

## Why
A public method that dereferences a null argument throws
`NullReferenceException` deep inside — the caller gets a stack trace pointing
at your internals instead of at their bug. Fail fast at the boundary, naming
the offending parameter.

## Do this
1. At the top of every public method, guard reference-type parameters:
   `ArgumentNullException.ThrowIfNull(name);` — or `ThrowIfNullOrEmpty` /
   `ThrowIfNullOrWhiteSpace` for strings.
2. Do it before any other logic — the guard is the first thing the method
   does.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by sprinkling `?.` and `??` through the method body instead —
  that turns a caller bug into silent wrong behavior downstream; throw at the
  boundary.
- Do not guard only *some* parameters because "that one is never null" — the
  rule is about the contract; validate all of them.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
