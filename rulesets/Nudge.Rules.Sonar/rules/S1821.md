# S1821 — "switch" statements should not be nested

## Why
A `switch` inside a `case` multiplies the reader's branch-tracking load: the inner switch's `break`s and the outer's interleave visually, and reasoning about fall-through and exhaustiveness breaks down across the two levels.

## Do this
1. Extract the inner `switch` into a private method named for what it decides.
2. If both switches dispatch on related data, consider a `switch` expression, a dictionary of handlers, or polymorphism instead of nested branching.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by converting the inner switch to an if/else chain — same nesting depth, same cognitive load, different keywords.
- Do not flatten by merging both switches' conditions into one giant condition — that just moves the complexity into S1067 territory.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
