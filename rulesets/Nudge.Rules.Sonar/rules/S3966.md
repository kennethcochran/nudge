# S3966 — Objects should not be disposed more than once

## Why
Calling `Dispose()` twice on the same object — an explicit `Dispose()` plus a
`using` statement, or disposal in both a `finally` block and the owning code —
is at best redundant and at worst triggers `ObjectDisposedException` or a
double-release of native resources in implementations that aren't idempotent.

## Do this
1. Give ownership to exactly one construct: keep the `using` statement (or
   declaration) and delete the manual `Dispose()` call, or vice versa.
2. Trace every path that touches the object and confirm exactly one of them
   disposes it.

## AVOID
- Do not wrap the second `Dispose()` in `try/catch (ObjectDisposedException)`
  to make the double-dispose "safe". Swallowing the exception hides the
  ownership confusion instead of fixing it — decide who owns disposal and
  dispose exactly once.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
