# S5542 — Encryption algorithms should be used with secure mode and padding scheme

## Why
The flagged code uses a broken or leaky configuration: DES/3DES/RC2 (brute-forceable or cryptanalyzed), ECB mode (identical plaintext blocks produce identical ciphertext blocks, leaking structure), or missing/weak padding. The ciphertext may look encrypted while revealing patterns — or the key may simply be crackable.

## Do this
1. Switch to AES (`Aes.Create()`) with CBC or, preferably, GCM mode and PKCS7 padding.
2. Generate a fresh random IV per encryption and store/transmit it alongside the ciphertext; never use a fixed or zero IV.
3. Derive keys from passwords with a KDF (`Rfc2898DeriveBytes`/PBKDF2, Argon2, or scrypt) — never `SHA256(password)` bytes or raw UTF-8 bytes.

## AVOID
- Do not keep the weak algorithm but rename the variable or wrap it in a helper — the cipher is still broken.
- Do not "upgrade" from ECB to CBC while hardcoding the IV to zeros — a fixed IV leaks whether two messages share a prefix.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
