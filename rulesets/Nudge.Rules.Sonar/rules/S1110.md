# S1110 — Redundant pairs of parentheses should be removed

## Why
Extra parentheses change neither meaning nor precedence — they are visual noise
that slows down every reader and can mislead someone into thinking precedence
was deliberately overridden where it wasn't.

## Do this
1. Remove the redundant parentheses the diagnostic points at.
2. Where precedence genuinely matters and is non-obvious, keep those
   parentheses — or better, extract the sub-expression into a named local so
   the grouping documents itself.
3. Rebuild; the compiler treats the two forms identically, so this is
   behavior-preserving by construction.

## AVOID
- This rule is purely mechanical and has no meaningful dodge: the complete
   fix is removing the redundant parentheses at the flagged location.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
