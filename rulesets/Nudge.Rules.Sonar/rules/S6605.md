# S6605 — Collection-specific "Exists" method should be used instead of the "Any" extension

## Why
Calling `.Any(predicate)` on a `List<T>` routes through LINQ's generic
`IEnumerable<T>` path, while `List<T>.Exists(predicate)` is the type's own
method — same semantics, less overhead, and it says exactly what it does on
that collection.

## Do this
1. Replace `.Any(predicate)` with `.Exists(predicate)` where the receiver is
   a `List<T>`.
2. Keep LINQ's `Any` for `IEnumerable<T>` sources that have no `Exists`
   method.
3. Rebuild; the behavior is identical by construction.

## AVOID
- This rule is purely mechanical and has no meaningful dodge: the complete
   fix is the method swap the diagnostic names.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
