# S109 — Magic numbers should not be used

## Why
A bare numeric literal (`if (retries > 3)`, `Timeout = 30000`) hides its
meaning: a reader can't tell what the number represents, and changing it in
one place misses the other places that duplicated it. Named constants turn the
number into documentation.

## Do this
1. Introduce a named constant whose name says what the number *means*
   (`private const int MaxRetries = 3;`), not what it is.
2. Replace every use of that literal with the constant.
3. Leave `-1`, `0`, and `1` alone — the rule exempts them by default.

## AVOID
- Do not introduce a constants bag that merely renames the list
   (`Constants.Number3 = 3`) — a constant named after its value explains
   nothing and just moves the magic number. Name it for its meaning.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
