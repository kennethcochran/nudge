# S3973 — A conditionally executed single line should be denoted by indentation

## Why
Without braces, indentation is the only signal of what a condition governs.
When the single statement under an `if` is not indented — or the statement
after it is indented to match — a reader cannot tell which lines are
conditional. This is the exact shape of the "goto fail" bug: unconditional
code that looks conditional.

## Do this
1. Preferred: add braces around the conditional statement. Braces are
   unambiguous; indentation is a hint.
2. Alternatively, indent the single conditional statement exactly one level,
   and keep the following statement at the outer level so the boundary is
   visible.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" the diagnostic by indenting the following statement to match
  the conditional one. That makes unconditional code look conditional —
  the very misreading this rule exists to prevent.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
