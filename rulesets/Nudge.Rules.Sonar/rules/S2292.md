# S2292 — Trivial properties should be auto-implemented

## Why
A property with an explicit backing field that does nothing but get and set is ceremony without function: the field adds a place for future divergence that currently means nothing, and readers check it for logic that isn't there.

## Do this
1. Replace it with an auto-property (`public int Count { get; set; }`), keeping the original accessibility and attributes.
2. If the backing field is referenced elsewhere (it shouldn't be, for a trivial property), reroute those references to the property.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by adding validation or logging to the setter just to make the backing field "non-trivial" — don't invent behavior to satisfy the analyzer.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
