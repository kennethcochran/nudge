# S4457 — Parameter validation in "async"/"await" methods should be wrapped

## Why
In an `async` method, `throw` for a bad argument doesn't fire when the method is called — it fires when the returned `Task` is awaited. Callers that validate eagerly, or that never await, see the failure late or never, which breaks the fail-fast contract argument validation is supposed to provide.

## Do this
1. Split the method: keep the public name/signature on a non-async method that validates arguments and throws synchronously.
2. Have it delegate to a private `async` core method that does the real work.
3. Callers are unaffected — same signature, but validation now throws at call time.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by validating inside the async method and documenting "throws on await" — the deferred-throw contract is the bug.
- Do not wrap the call site in try/catch instead — that patches one caller, not the method.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
