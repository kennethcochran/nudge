# S1109 — A close curly brace should be located at the beginning of a line

## Why
This codebase uses Allman brace style: block boundaries sit at the start of
their own lines so the eye can scan them vertically. A closing brace tucked
at the end of a code line (`...; }`) breaks that scan and hides where blocks
end, especially in deeply nested code.

## Do this
1. Move the closing brace to the beginning of its own line, aligned with the
   opening statement.

## AVOID
- This rule is mechanical: reformat the brace. Do not "fix" it by collapsing
  the entire block onto a single line — that trades a style violation for
  unreadable code.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
