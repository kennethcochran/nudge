# S2222 — Locks should be released on all paths

## Why
`Monitor.Enter` without a matching `Exit` on every path — including exception
paths — leaves the lock held forever, and every other thread waiting on it
deadlocks. This is exactly the bug the `lock` statement exists to prevent.

## Do this
1. Replace manual `Monitor.Enter`/`Exit` with a `lock (obj) { ... }` statement —
   it emits the try/finally for you.
2. If you must use `Monitor` directly (e.g. `TryEnter` with a timeout), put
   `Enter` immediately before a `try` and `Exit` in the `finally`, with nothing
   that can throw in between.
3. Never `await` inside a `lock` — switch to `SemaphoreSlim` for async code.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by adding a `Monitor.Exit` at the bottom of the method
  without a `try`/`finally` — any exception before it still leaks the lock.
- Do not wrap the body in try/catch that swallows exceptions just to reach
  the `Exit` — use `finally`; swallowing hides the original failure.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
