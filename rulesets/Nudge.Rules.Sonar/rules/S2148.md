# S2148 — Underscores should be used to make large numbers readable

## Why
`1000000000` vs `10000000000` differ by one zero — invisible at a glance.
Digit separators make magnitude errors reviewable: `1_000_000_000` vs
`10_000_000_000` can't be confused.

## Do this
1. Add `_` separators grouping digits in threes from the right: `1_000_000`.
2. For fractional parts, group from the decimal point outward: `3.141_59`.
3. Apply to hex and binary literals too (`0xFF_FF00`), grouping in fours where
   that matches the domain (e.g. bytes, nibbles).
4. Cover every large literal the diagnostic flags — don't leave a mix of
   separated and unseparated literals in the same expression.

## AVOID
- This rule is mechanical — there is no meaningful dodge. Just get the
  grouping right: separators go every three digits from the right, so
  `10_00_000` groups wrong and misleads worse than no separators at all.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
