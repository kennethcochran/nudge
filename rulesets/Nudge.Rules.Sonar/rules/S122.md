# S122 — Statements should be on separate lines

## Why
`x = Compute(); y = x * 2;` on one line compresses two operations into a
single visual unit — harder to scan, harder to set breakpoints on, and diffs
touch both statements when only one of them changed.

## Do this
1. Put each statement on its own line.
2. While you're at it, check whether the statements are actually related
   enough to have shared a line — usually they aren't, which is the point.

## AVOID
- This one is purely mechanical: one statement per line is the whole fix. Do
  not "fix" it by joining the statements with a comma operator or rewriting
  them as a single expression-bodied member that keeps two operations on one
  line — the line layout is the issue, not the statement count.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
