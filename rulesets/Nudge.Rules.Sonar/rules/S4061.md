# S4061 — "params" should be used instead of "varargs"

## Why
__arglist (varargs) is a C++/CLI-era interop hack: not CLS-compliant,
invisible to most languages, and awkward from reflection-based APIs. params
object[] (or a typed params array) does the same job inside the language.
This rule flags varargs usage.

## Do this
1. Replace the __arglist parameter with params object[] — or params T[] if the elements share a type.
2. Update call sites from Foo(__arglist(a, b)) to Foo(a, b).
3. If the varargs exist for native interop, isolate the interop signature and expose a params-based managed wrapper over it.

## AVOID
- Do not keep __arglist and add a params overload that forwards to it — the non-compliant signature remains public.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
