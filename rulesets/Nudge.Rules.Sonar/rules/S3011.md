# S3011 — Reflection should not be used to increase accessibility of classes, methods, or fields

## Why
Using reflection (`BindingFlags.NonPublic`,
`GetMethod` on privates, `SetValue` on private fields) to reach around
a type's accessibility breaks its encapsulation contract: private means
"free to change without notice", and reflective callers turn every
internal refactor into a potential runtime `MissingMethodException`.
It also defeats visibility-based security boundaries.

## Do this
1. Stop reaching through reflection. Expose what you need through a
   real API: a public/internal member, an interface, or
   `InternalsVisibleTo` for test assemblies.
2. If the member must stay private and you need it in tests, test
   through the public surface — the test then verifies behavior, not
   implementation.
3. Remove the `BindingFlags.NonPublic` lookup entirely once the proper
   API exists.

## AVOID
- Do not cache the reflected `MethodInfo`/`FieldInfo` in a static
  field to "reduce the cost" — the accessibility bypass is the problem,
  not the performance.
- Do not switch to `dynamic` either; it bypasses the same boundary with
  less ceremony.

- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
