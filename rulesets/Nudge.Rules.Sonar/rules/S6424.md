# S6424 — Interfaces for durable entities should satisfy the restrictions

## Why
Durable Entities replay serialized operations against the entity interface,
so the interface must obey the framework's contract: supported method
signatures only, serializable parameters, no properties, events, or generics.
A non-conforming member compiles fine and then fails at runtime, when the
dispatcher cannot route or deserialize the call.

## Do this
1. Check each flagged member against the durable-entities interface
   restrictions (method-only, supported signatures, serializable types).
2. Reshape the member to fit: expose state through explicit get/set methods
   with serializable types instead of properties, remove generics, keep
   signatures simple.
3. Re-run the entity's operations end-to-end; interface violations surface at
   dispatch time, not compile time.

## AVOID
- Do not work around the rule by casting to the concrete implementation to
  reach the non-conforming member. The replay machinery dispatches through
  the interface contract — the back door breaks the moment the entity is
  rehydrated or replayed.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
