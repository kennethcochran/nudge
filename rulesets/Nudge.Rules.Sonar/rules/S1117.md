# S1117 — Local variables should not shadow class fields or properties

## Why
A local named the same as a field means the bare name silently refers to the
local, not the field — assignments meant for the field update the local
instead, and readers can't tell which one a line touches without scrolling.
Shadowing bugs survive review because the code looks right.

## Do this
1. Rename the local to something describing its narrower role (`name` →
   `displayName`, `candidateName`), keeping the field's name stable since
   it's the wider contract.
2. If the local genuinely *is* the field's value in progress, assign through
   the field directly or use a clearly temporary name.
3. After renaming, check for `this.` qualifiers that were masking the
   shadowing — remove the now-unneeded ones, or keep the codebase's
   convention consistently.

## AVOID
- Do not rename the *field* to accommodate the local — fields are the
  broader contract; locals are the disposable names.
- Do not keep the shadowing and sprinkle `this.` on field accesses; the next
  editor will forget one, and the bug returns.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
