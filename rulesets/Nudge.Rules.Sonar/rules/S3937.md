# S3937 — Number patterns should be regular

## Why
Irregular digit grouping (`100_00`, `1_0000_000`) defeats the purpose of separators: instead of making magnitude scannable, the odd groups make the value *harder* to verify at a glance — which is exactly where wrong-constant bugs hide.

## Do this
1. Regroup decimal digits in consistent thousands: `10_000`, `1_000_000`.
2. For hex/binary literals, group by the natural unit (e.g. bytes: `0xFF_FF_FF_FF`).

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by removing the underscores entirely — separators are good; make them regular instead of deleting them.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
