# S6566 — Use "DateTimeOffset" instead of "DateTime"

## Why
A `DateTime` carries no offset — `2026-10-02 12:00` with `Kind.Unspecified`
is ambiguous across time zones, and serialization round-trips can silently
shift the instant. `DateTimeOffset` pins the moment unambiguously, which is
what timestamps, deadlines, and audit fields actually are.

## Do this
1. Change the type to `DateTimeOffset` for anything representing an instant
   in time (timestamps, expirations, audit fields).
2. Convert at the boundaries: parse and accept with offset awareness
   (`DateTimeOffset.Parse`); only drop to `.UtcDateTime` where a UTC
   `DateTime` is truly required.
3. Keep `DateTime` (or `DateOnly`) only for things that genuinely aren't
   instants: birthdays, wall-clock schedule times.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by keeping `DateTime` and stamping `DateTimeKind.Utc`
  everywhere — the ambiguity survives serialization and every consumer must
  still trust the convention; change the type.
- Do not convert with `.ToUniversalTime()` on an `Unspecified` kind and call
  it done — that assumes local time; the bug just moves.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
