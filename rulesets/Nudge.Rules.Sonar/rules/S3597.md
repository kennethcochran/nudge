# S3597 — "ServiceContract" and "OperationContract" attributes should be used together

## Why
`[ServiceContract]` without `[OperationContract]` exposes a service with no
operations; `[OperationContract]` on a method of something that is not a
`[ServiceContract]` marks an operation nobody can call. Either way the
declared contract silently differs from the intended one, and the mismatch
surfaces at runtime or in generated proxies.

## Do this
1. Decide what the service contract actually is.
2. Apply `[ServiceContract]` to the contract interface and
   `[OperationContract]` to each real operation — and only the real
   operations.
3. Remove the stray attribute wherever no contract was intended.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" a missing `[OperationContract]` by tagging every method on
  the interface, including helpers. Mark only the operations clients should
  call; helpers do not belong on the wire contract.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
