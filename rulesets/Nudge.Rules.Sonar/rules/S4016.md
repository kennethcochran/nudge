# S4016 — Enumeration members should not be named "Reserved"

## Why
A member named `Reserved` claims a numeric slot for future use, but nothing
enforces the claim: anyone can cast that value today, and serializers and
formatters will happily round-trip it. The "reservation" is a fiction that
invites exactly the collision it pretends to prevent.

## Do this
1. Remove the `Reserved` member.
2. Reserve the numeric range honestly: start the next real member at the
   desired value with a comment explaining the gap, or document the reserved
   range where the enum is defined.

## AVOID
- Do not rename it to `Reserved1`, `Unused`, or `DoNotUse`. A new spelling
  does not make the claim enforceable — it is the same fiction under a
  different name.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
