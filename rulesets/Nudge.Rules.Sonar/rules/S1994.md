# S1994 — "for" loop increment clauses should modify the loops' counters

## Why
`for (int i = 0; i < n; j++)` increments the wrong variable — the loop either
never terminates or terminates for the wrong reason. The increment clause
exists to advance the counter the condition tests; when it touches something
else, the header lies about the loop's mechanics.

## Do this
1. Make the increment clause modify the loop counter: `i++`.
2. If the counter is genuinely advanced inside the body (unusual but
   intentional), say so with a comment and consider a `while` loop instead —
   `while` doesn't promise the increment in its header.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by adding `i++` inside the body while leaving the bogus
  increment clause in the header — the header still lies; fix the clause or
  switch to `while`.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
