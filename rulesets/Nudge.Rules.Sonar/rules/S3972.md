# S3972 — Conditionals should start on new lines

## Why
`if (x) DoWork();` on a single line visually demotes the branch to a footnote —
during review and debugging the conditional logic is easy to misread or miss
entirely, especially as the line grows.

## Do this
1. Put the `if` / `else if` / `else` on its own line and the body on the
   following line(s), wrapped in braces.
2. Apply the same treatment to the whole conditional chain so the structure
   is scannable at a glance.

## AVOID
- Do not keep everything on one line and just add braces
  (`if (x) { DoWork(); }`). The rule is about line layout, not braces — the
  conditional and its body must start on new lines.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
