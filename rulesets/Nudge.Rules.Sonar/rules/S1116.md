# S1116 — Empty statements should be removed

## Why
A stray `;` is almost always a typo — and after an `if`, `while`, or `for` it silently detaches the body from the condition, turning a guarded statement into an unconditional one. That's a logic bug wearing the disguise of a style nit.

## Do this
1. Delete the stray semicolon.
2. If it sits directly after a control statement (`if (x);`), inspect what follows: check whether the next block was meant to be conditional, and restore the intended logic — don't just delete the `;` and move on.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by joining the empty statement onto the `if` line to hide it — the detached-body logic bug remains.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
