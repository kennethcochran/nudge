# S2479 — Whitespace and control characters in string literals should be explicit

## Why
A literal tab, carriage return, or control character inside a string literal
is invisible in review — a reader cannot tell a tab from spaces, spot a
stray `\r`, or distinguish a non-breaking space from a regular one. Explicit
escapes (`\t`, `\n`, `\u00A0`) say exactly which character you mean.

## Do this
1. Replace literal whitespace and control characters with escape sequences.
2. For intentionally multiline text, prefer raw string literals where the
   layout is visible and deliberate.
3. For non-breaking spaces and other lookalikes, always use the `\u` escape
   — never a pasted invisible character.

## AVOID
- Do not move the literal whitespace into a `const string` elsewhere. The
  invisibility moves with it; the next reader is just as blind.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
