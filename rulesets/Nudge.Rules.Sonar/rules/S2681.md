# S2681 — Multiline blocks should be enclosed in curly braces

## Why
An unbraced multi-line `if` body is a trap for the next edit: someone adds a
line intending it to be conditional, the indentation lies, and the statement
silently falls outside the condition. Braces make the scope explicit and
future edits safe.

## Do this
1. Wrap the multi-line block in `{ }`.
2. Keep one statement per line inside the block.
3. Let the formatter confirm the scope matches the indentation — if they
   disagree, the braces (not the whitespace) are now the truth.

## AVOID
- Do not collapse the body onto a single line to dodge the rule — a long
  single-line body trades readability for nothing and the next multi-line
  edit reintroduces the hazard.
- Do not add braces to only the flagged block while leaving identical unbraced
  blocks nearby — fix the pattern everywhere it appears.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
