# S6563 — Use UTC when recording DateTime instants

## Why
`DateTime.Now` records local time: DST transitions make some instants ambiguous or nonexistent, and comparing or storing local times across machines in different zones corrupts ordering. Anything that records *when something happened* — audit fields, log timestamps, expiry calculations — must be zone-independent.

## Do this
1. Replace `DateTime.Now` with `DateTime.UtcNow` for recorded instants.
2. Convert to local time only at the display boundary, never in storage or logic.
3. If you need the offset carried with the value, use `DateTimeOffset.UtcNow`.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by calling `.ToUniversalTime()` on `DateTime.Now` at the use site — same value, and now every reader must verify the conversion; capture UTC at the source.
- Do not keep `DateTime.Now` "because the server is in one timezone" — servers move, and containers don't promise a zone.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
