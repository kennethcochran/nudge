# S3926 — Deserialization methods should be provided for "OptionalField" members

## Why
`[OptionalField]` marks a field added in a later version of a serializable
type. When old serialized data (which lacks the field) is deserialized, the
member is left at its default value — silently wrong state, not an error. An
`[OnDeserialized]` method is the hook that initializes it to something sane.

## Do this
1. Add an `[OnDeserialized]` method that sets a sensible default for the
   `[OptionalField]` member when it wasn't present in the stream.
2. Better still: migrate off `BinaryFormatter` entirely — it's deprecated and
   blocked by default — to a version-tolerant serializer
   (`System.Text.Json`), where missing members get defaults naturally and
   `[OptionalField]` isn't needed.
3. Test by deserializing an old payload and asserting the member's value.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by removing `[OptionalField]` — that breaks deserialization
   of old payloads outright (missing-field exception) instead of leaving a
   default. Provide the initialization, or migrate serializers.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
