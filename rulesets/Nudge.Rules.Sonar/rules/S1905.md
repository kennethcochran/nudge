# S1905 — Redundant casts should not be used

## Why
A cast that doesn't change the type adds noise and implies a conversion is happening where none is — every reader wastes effort checking for a narrowing or widening that isn't there. It's also a smell that the surrounding typing drifted and nobody noticed.

## Do this
1. Delete the cast.
2. If removing it changes overload resolution or type inference, fix the underlying typing instead of re-adding the cast — the surprise is the real problem.

## AVOID
- Do not replace the cast with `as` + `!` or a double cast through `object` — same noise, more characters. Deletion is the complete fix for this rule.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
