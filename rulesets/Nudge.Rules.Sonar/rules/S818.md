# S818 — Literal suffixes should be upper case

## Why
Lowercase `l` is visually identical to `1` in most fonts — `0l` reads as `01` at a glance. Uppercase suffixes remove the misreading entirely. This rule is purely mechanical: the fix is the whole story.

## Do this
1. Change the suffix to uppercase: `0L`, `1F`, `2D`, `3M`, `4UL`, and so on.
2. Apply it consistently to every numeric literal in the flagged locations.

## AVOID
- No meaningful dodge exists for this rule — uppercasing the suffix is the complete fix; there is nothing to game.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
