# S4456 — Parameter validation in yielding methods should be wrapped

## Why
An iterator method — one containing yield — executes none of its code,
including argument validation, until the first MoveNext(). So constructing the
enumerable with a bad argument appears to succeed, and the ArgumentNullException surfaces later, far from the call, when the sequence is
first enumerated. This rule flags parameter checks inside iterators.

## Do this
1. Split the method: a public non-iterator wrapper that validates arguments eagerly and throws immediately, delegating to a private iterator that does the yielding.
2. Keep all validation in the wrapper — the iterator body should be able to assume its inputs are already checked.
3. Name the private iterator to make the split obvious (e.g. GetItemsCore) so the next reader doesn't 'simplify' the two back together.

## AVOID
- Do not move the validation to after the first yield — it still runs lazily, just later.
- Do not rely on callers enumerating immediately; deferred execution is the caller's prerogative and the bug is in the callee.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
