# S3363 — Date and time should not be used as a type for primary keys

## Why
`DateTime` keys collide: two rows can share a tick, clocks get adjusted, and different database providers store different precisions — so "unique" timestamps aren't unique and comparisons behave inconsistently. Primary keys must be unique and stable; timestamps are neither.

## Do this
1. Add a surrogate key (integer identity or `Guid`) as the primary key.
2. Keep the timestamp as a regular, possibly indexed, column for ordering and queries.
3. Migrate existing data: backfill the new key, then switch the PK over.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by switching `DateTime` to `DateTimeOffset` or increasing precision — the collision and clock-skew problems remain.
- Do not concatenate the timestamp with another column into a composite key just to force uniqueness — use a real surrogate key.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
