# S3235 — Redundant parentheses should not be used

## Why
`return ((x + y));` adds visual noise that sends readers hunting for a
precedence subtlety that isn't there — and over time, a codebase full of
meaningless parens teaches the team to stop trusting that parentheses mean
something.

## Do this
1. Remove the redundant parentheses. Keep the ones that actually disambiguate
   precedence or genuinely aid readability in mixed expressions.

## AVOID
- This one is purely mechanical: deleting the extra parentheses is the
  complete fix. Do not "fix" it by reformatting the expression across multiple
  lines around the redundant parens — the parentheses are still redundant;
  remove them.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
