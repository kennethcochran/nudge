# S4027 — Exceptions should provide standard constructors

## Why
The .NET ecosystem constructs exceptions reflectively — serializers,
`Activator`, and catch-and-rethrow patterns all expect the standard
constructor set. A custom exception missing them breaks `throw new
MyException("msg", inner)` call sites and any tooling that rehydrates
exceptions.

## Do this
1. Add the three standard constructors: parameterless, `(string message)`,
   and `(string message, Exception innerException)`, each chaining to the
   matching base constructor.
2. On modern .NET (8+), do not add the binary-serialization constructor —
   it is obsolete; the rule's intent is the usable set, not the legacy one.

## AVOID
- Do not add the constructors with `throw new NotImplementedException()`
  bodies to satisfy the shape check. A constructor that explodes on use is
  worse than a missing one — every construction site becomes a landmine.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
