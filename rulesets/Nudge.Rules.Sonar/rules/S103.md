# S103 — Lines should not be too long

## Why
Overlong lines force horizontal scrolling and hide the end of the statement — where the important argument or the closing parenthesis lives — from code review. What reviewers can't see, they can't check.

## Do this
1. Break the line at a natural boundary: after commas in argument lists, around operators, or by extracting a sub-expression into a named local.
2. If a string literal is the culprit, consider whether it belongs in a resource, a named constant, or a multi-line construction — don't just shove it somewhere.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by raising the limit in `.editorconfig` — changing the threshold (and `.editorconfig` severity overrides are forbidden outright) doesn't make the line readable.
- Do not break a string literal mid-value in a way that changes what it contains.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
