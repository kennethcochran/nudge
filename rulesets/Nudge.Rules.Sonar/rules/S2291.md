# S2291 — Overflow checking should not be disabled for "Enumerable.Sum"

## Why
`Enumerable.Sum` over integral types silently wraps on overflow inside an
`unchecked` context. Disabling overflow checking around a sum trades a loud,
catchable `OverflowException` for a quietly wrong total — the worst kind of
financial or statistical bug, because everything downstream looks plausible.

## Do this
1. Remove the `unchecked` around the `Sum`, or wrap it in `checked` so
   overflow surfaces immediately.
2. If overflow is a realistic possibility, widen the accumulator first
   (sum into `long` or `decimal`) rather than disabling the check.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it by casting the result to a wider type after the sum. The
  wrap already happened inside the aggregation — widening the damaged
  result does not undamage it.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
