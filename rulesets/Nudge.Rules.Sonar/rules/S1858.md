# S1858 — "ToString()" calls should not be redundant

## Why
String interpolation (`$"{x}"`), `string.Concat`, `StringBuilder.Append`, and
`Console.WriteLine` already call `ToString()` on their arguments — writing
`x.ToString()` explicitly is noise that adds nothing and slightly obscures
what the expression produces.

## Do this
1. Remove the redundant `.ToString()` calls the diagnostic flags.
2. Rebuild; the calls were already happening implicitly, so this is
   behavior-preserving by construction.

## AVOID
- This rule is purely mechanical and has no meaningful dodge: the complete
   fix is removing the redundant call at the flagged location.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
