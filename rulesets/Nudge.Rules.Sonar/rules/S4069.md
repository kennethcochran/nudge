# S4069 — Operator overloads should have named alternatives

## Why
Operator overloads (`+`, `==`, …) are invisible to
languages and callers that don't use operator syntax, and they're
undiscoverable in IntelliSense when you're looking for "how do I add
these". A named alternative method (`Add`, `Equals`, …) makes the
operation callable and discoverable everywhere.

## Do this
1. Add a public method with a descriptive name that performs exactly
   the operation the operator performs.
2. Implement one in terms of the other (operator delegates to the
   method, or vice versa) so the two can never drift apart.
3. For equality operators, the named alternative is `Equals` — and it
   brings `GetHashCode` obligations with it.

## AVOID
- Do not add a named method with subtly different semantics than the
  operator (e.g. `Add` that validates while `operator+` doesn't) — the
  alternative must do exactly what the operator does, or callers get two
  different "adds".

- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
