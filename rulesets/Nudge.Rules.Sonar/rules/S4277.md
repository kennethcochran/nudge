# S4277 — "Shared" parts should not be created with "new"

## Why
A "shared" (singleton-lifetime) part exists so the whole composition shares
one instance with one state. Constructing it with `new` creates an
unsanctioned second copy: its state diverges from the container's instance,
and any component holding your copy sees a different world than everyone
else.

## Do this
1. Resolve the part through the container or composition root instead of
   constructing it.
2. If the call site cannot reach the container, that is a design smell —
   pass the shared instance in (constructor injection) rather than building
   a spare.

## AVOID
- Do not "share" your `new` instance by parking it in a static field. That
  is a hand-rolled singleton sitting beside the container's singleton: the
  container still does not know about it, and now there are two sources of
  truth for "the" instance.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`. Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
