# S2342 — Enumeration types should comply with a naming convention

## Why
Names like `UserEnum`, `FlagsStatus`, or a `[Flags]` enum in the singular
mislead every reader about whether bitwise semantics apply. The convention —
no `Enum`/`Flag` affixes, singular for plain enums, plural for `[Flags]` —
makes the intent visible at the declaration instead of requiring a hunt for
the attribute.

## Do this
1. Drop the `Enum`/`Flags` prefix or suffix from the name.
2. Use the singular form for plain enums (`UserRole`), the plural form for
   `[Flags]` enums (`UserRoles`).
3. Rename with the IDE's rename refactoring so every reference, serialization
   name, and switch label follows.

## AVOID
- Do not keep the old name around as an `[Obsolete]` alias "for
  compatibility" — two names for one concept is the confusion this rule
  exists to remove.
- Do not rename only the enum while leaving a mismatched `Enum`-suffixed
  variable or property name behind — the convention applies to the concept,
  not just the declaration line.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
