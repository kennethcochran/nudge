# S5122 — Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) policy should be restricted to trusted origins

## Why
`AllowAnyOrigin()` combined with `AllowCredentials()` tells browsers to hand
your cookies and auth headers to any website the victim visits — a malicious
page can then drive your API as that user. A wildcard origin is only safe when
no credentials are involved at all.

## Do this
1. Replace the wildcard with an explicit list of trusted origins:
   `WithOrigins("https://app.example.com")`.
2. Call `AllowCredentials()` only for origins on that list; leave it off
   otherwise.
3. If origins must be dynamic (multi-tenant), validate each against an
   allowlist inside `SetIsOriginAllowed` — never by echoing the request's
   `Origin` header.

## AVOID
- Do not "fix" it with `SetIsOriginAllowed(_ => true)` or by echoing
  `Request.Headers["Origin"]` back — that's `AllowAnyOrigin` with extra steps,
  still sending credentials anywhere.
- Do not keep `AllowAnyOrigin` and drop `AllowCredentials` while the frontend
  still needs cookies — you'll just break auth and revert later; scope the
  origins properly instead.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
