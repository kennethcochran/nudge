# S138 — Functions should not have too many lines of code

## Why
A 200-line method is several responsibilities wearing one name — untestable in
isolation, hard to review, and every change risks unrelated behavior. Length is
the symptom; mixed responsibilities are the disease.

## Do this
1. Identify the distinct steps inside the method and extract each into a
   well-named private method that does one thing.
2. Name the extracted methods after what they accomplish (`ValidateOrder`,
   `BuildResponseHeaders`), not their position (`DoPart1`).
3. Re-check the result: each remaining method should have a single reason to
   change.

## AVOID
- Do not split the method arbitrarily (cut at line 100, name the halves
  `ProcessA` / `ProcessB`) just to get under the limit. The complexity and
  the mixed responsibilities move unchanged into two methods — the metric
  drops, the problem doesn't.
- Do not suppress with `#pragma warning disable` or `[SuppressMessage]`.
  Suppression of analyzer diagnostics is forbidden by the project's AGENTS.md.
